**Shared tap behavior and app-bug boundary**

`tapScrollControl()` uses only the installed TesterArmy APIs. It scopes discovery to the supplied scroll view or collection view. It checks that the anchor has stopped moving, reads the scroll-view and floating-tab-bar bounds, and makes short swipes within the unobstructed content area. It requires the full anchor to fit above the tab bar with a margin. It verifies that the tap point lies inside both the content and the actual button, then uses `button.tap({ position })` at that measured point. For an oversized session card, the source label supplies the anchor; the whole card does not need to fit in the viewport.

The installed `@e2e-dev/mobile` sends a plain locator tap through `press({ ref })`. The installed `e2e` sends a positioned tap through its pointer API. The saved no-navigation traces establish that the earlier ref taps did not reach their intended destination. They do not identify the exact internal driver failure. The measured pointer change still requires real UI navigation and leaves all destination assertions in place.

The helper neither skips failed controls nor catches and ignores tap errors. If bounded scrolling cannot clear a control, it fails with the last anchor, button, scroll-view, and tab-bar bounds. A control that remains blocked at the end must remain a failure for an APP BUG review. The current covered-element captures stopped at 46%, 79%, and 53%; none proves that end-of-scroll condition. The no-navigation captures also do not prove that a user's tap on the visible button fails. **No APP BUG is confirmed in this round; runtime verification remains pending.**
